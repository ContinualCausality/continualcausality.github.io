# Call for Participation

> All submissions are handled through OpenReview: [novel works](https://openreview.net/group?id=AAAI.org/2027/Bridge/Continual_Causality_Novel), [previously published](https://openreview.net/group?id=AAAI.org/2027/Bridge/Continual_Causality_Previously_Published)

> Submission deadline (novel work): **December 1, 2026**, Anywhere On Earth (AoE).

> Notifications (novel work): **December 11, 2026** 

> Submission deadline (previously published work): **January 8, 2027**, Anywhere On Earth (AoE).

> Notifications (previously published work): **January 15, 2027**


> Bridge: **February 17, 2027**


The fields of **causality** and **continual learning** investigate complementary aspects of human cognition, and artificial intelligence must emulate both if it is to reason and generalize in complex environments. On the one hand, causality theory provides the language, algorithms, and tools to discover and infer cause-and-effect relationships from data. On the other hand, continual learning systems must balance learning from new data as they become available with retaining previous knowledge of systems that may not be stationary. Our recurring **Continual Causality Bridge** continues working towards a unified treatment of these fields by providing a **space to learn and discuss**, and to **connect** and **build a diverse long-term community**.
This year, we invite submissions for two non-archival tracks: vision papers of 2 pages and short papers of 4 pages on novel work due on December 1, 2026, and full length papers of previously published works, due on January 8, 2027.

### Non-archival track: novel works
We invite submissions that present general positions or visions of how to link the two fields, outline challenges that need to be overcome, highlight synergies, propose tangible future steps, or discuss first practical approaches and solutions to relevant problems. Our vision is for prospective community members to voice diverse views that have the potential to advance AI through an ongoing interdisciplinary exchange. We therefore have no strict constraints on the exact sub-topics of submissions, as long as they target the overall goal of bridging the fields. Submissions are free to focus on a single particularly interesting synergy, take an angle from a specific scientific discipline, or sketch a grander view. To provide some suggestions, contributions could center around the following aspects, including but not limited to:

* Continual learning and exploitation of causal systems in dynamic non-stationary environments.
* Knowledge integration, contextualization, and unlearning in causal models under continuous, non-stationary streams of data.
* Leveraging causal structure for continual and non-stationary reinforcement learning and world models.
* Enabling agents to navigate, intervene, and learn in dynamic, confounded, and partially observable worlds.
* Shortcut learning and confounding in non-stationary data.
* Foundation models at the intersection of causal and continual learning.
* Datasets and benchmarks, vision and position papers, and applications connected to continual causality.

**Works must be original and limited to two pages (vision paper) or four pages (short paper)** (excluding references and optional appendices) in the AAAI format. The **deadline for submissions is December 1, 2026 (AoE)**. All submissions will be managed through [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/group?id=AAAI.org/2027/Bridge/Continual_Causality_Novel). The **review process for novel works is double-blind**, so submissions should be **anonymized**. 
Accepted works will be uploaded to the continual causality website, but generally do not count as publications. They are free to be resubmitted to future venues. 

### Non-archival track: previously published works
In addition to novel works, we will also allow for **presentations of recently published works** for non-archival inclusion in our bridge. For already published works, we consider work recent if it has been published **at a top tier venue after AAAI-26** (including works that have been accepted to the **main track of AAAI-27**). This includes accepted works at conferences (for example, but not limited to NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, IJCAI, AISTATS, UAI) and journals (for example, but not limited to JMLR, JAIR, TMLR). These works should be submitted in their original length and template.

Submissions are handled using a separate [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/group?id=AAAI.org/2027/Bridge/Continual_Causality_Previously_Published) form and the submission deadline for the track for previously published works is **January 8 (AoE)**. **Submissions of previously accepted papers do not need to be anonymized**. As these works are already peer-reviewed, our review process will focus primarily on technical correctness and fit to the bridge’s themes.
